Privacy notice

How LedgerHouse handles customer data

LedgerHouse processes accounting, bookkeeping, tax, client-support and management-reporting data for businesses and their advisers.

Data may include company details, user details, uploaded invoices, receipts, bank files, accounting journals, VAT records, HMRC authorisation tokens and audit records.

Data is used to provide the service, support client accounting workflows, prepare reports and interact with HMRC only where authorised by the customer.

HMRC access uses OAuth authorisation. LedgerHouse does not store customers' HMRC sign-in passwords. Stored HMRC tokens are encrypted at application level.

Customer data is protected by authentication, tenant access controls, audit records, security headers and production HTTPS. Customers can request export, correction or deletion where legally applicable.

Retention and deletion

Accounting records, uploaded evidence, reports and audit logs are retained for the statutory accounting and tax period, unless a longer contract or legal obligation applies. Deletion requests are reviewed against accounting, tax, audit and fraud-prevention duties before data is removed.

Sub-processors and integrations

Where enabled, LedgerHouse may connect to HMRC, Companies House, payment providers, banking providers or hosting/database services. These services are used only for the purpose of delivering the accounting workflow, reporting, payment collection or compliance features selected by the customer or practice.

Security contact

Security concerns should be sent to info@kafs-ltd.com with the subject "LedgerHouse security report". Include the affected page, account, time observed and safe reproduction steps where available.
