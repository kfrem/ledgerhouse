Privacy notice

How LedgerHouse handles customer data

LedgerHouse processes accounting, bookkeeping, tax, client-support and management-reporting data for businesses and their advisers.

Data may include company details, user details, uploaded invoices, receipts, bank files, accounting journals, VAT records, HMRC authorisation tokens and audit records.

Data is used to provide the service, support client accounting workflows, prepare reports and interact with HMRC only where authorised by the customer.

HMRC access uses OAuth authorisation. LedgerHouse does not store customers' HMRC sign-in passwords. Stored HMRC tokens are encrypted at application level.

Customer data is protected by authentication, tenant access controls, audit records, security headers and production HTTPS. Customers can request export, correction or deletion where legally applicable.
